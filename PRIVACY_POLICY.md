# Privacy Policy for Local Lekker

**Last Updated: November 7, 2026**

## Introduction

Local Lekker ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Local Lekker mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By using Local Lekker, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Contact Information

- **Email**: support@locallekker.co.za
- **Company**: Local Lekker
- **Location**: South Africa

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information

When you register and use our App, we may collect:

- **Account Information**: Name, surname, email address, phone number, password
- **Payment Information**: Payment and transaction details. Card details are handled securely by Paystack and are not stored by us.
- **Business Information**: For business partners - business name, address, contact details, banking information
- **Profile Data**: Date of birth, gender, ethnicity (optional demographic information)

### 2. Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: App features used, time spent in app, interaction data
- **Location Data**: Approximate location to show nearby businesses (with your permission)
- **QR Code Data**: QR codes scanned for payment transactions

### 3. Camera and Photo Library

- **Camera Access**: Used for QR code scanning and capturing receipt images
- **Photo Library**: May be used to select receipt images and business logos
- **Receipt Data**: Receipt images and information extracted from receipts may be stored to provide receipt-processing and transaction-history features

**We do NOT** collect facial recognition data or use the camera for surveillance purposes.

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

1. **Account Management**: Creating and managing your membership account
2. **Payment Processing**: Facilitating transactions between members and businesses
3. **Service Delivery**: Providing QR code payment features and discount management
4. **Communication**: Sending transaction confirmations, account notifications, service updates
5. **Business Matching**: Showing you nearby local businesses based on location
6. **Fraud Prevention**: Detecting and preventing fraudulent transactions
7. **App Improvement**: Analyzing usage patterns to enhance user experience
8. **Legal Compliance**: Meeting regulatory and legal requirements

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Share Data With:

1. **Payment Processors**: Paystack (for secure payment processing)
2. **Cloud Services**: Supabase (for secure data storage and authentication)
3. **Business Partners**: Transaction details shared with businesses where you make payments
4. **Legal Authorities**: When required by law or to protect rights and safety

### We Do NOT:

- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data for advertising purposes
- Use your information for purposes other than those stated in this policy

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption**: All data transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption
- **Secure Storage**: Data stored on encrypted Supabase servers
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access restrictions
- **Payment Security**: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing via Paystack
- **Authentication**: Secure password hashing and authentication protocols

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

1. **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
2. **Correction**: Update or correct inaccurate information
3. **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and data
4. **Data Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
5. **Withdrawal**: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
6. **Objection**: Object to certain types of data processing

### How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at support@locallekker.co.za to:
- Access your data
- Update your information
- Delete your account
- Request data export
- Submit privacy concerns

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while your account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Data deleted after 90 days of account deactivation
- **Transaction Records**: Retained for 7 years for legal and tax compliance
- **Archived Data**: Anonymized for analytics after account deletion

## Children's Privacy

Local Lekker is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
