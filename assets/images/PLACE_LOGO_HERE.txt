## Logo Specifications

### Required:
- ✅ **Format:** PNG
- ✅ **File Name:** `creator_logo.png` (exact name, lowercase)
- ✅ **Size:** Recommended 120x120 pixels (or any small square/horizontal logo)

### Recommended:
- ✨ **Background:** Transparent PNG for best appearance
- ✨ **Style:** Simple, clean design that works at small sizes
- ✨ **Colors:** Will display as-is with opacity applied

## Quick Steps

1. **Get your logo ready** (PNG format, small size)
2. **Rename it to:** `creator_logo.png`
3. **Copy it here:** `assets/images/creator_logo.png`
4. **Run:** `flutter clean && flutter pub get`
5. **Restart your app** to see the watermark

## What Happens Next?

Once you place your logo here, it will automatically appear:
- ✅ At the bottom of the **Welcome Page** (already added as example)
- 📱 You can add it to other screens using the widgets in `lib/widgets/creator_watermark.dart`

## Don't Have a Logo Yet?

No problem! The app includes a fallback display:
- Shows a small "Logo" text box if the image file is missing
- Your app will still work perfectly while you prepare your logo
